亮黄与技术排版

高性能数据库界面以近纯黑为底，电光黄作为品牌强调色。白色粗体无衬线字、黄色主按钮和黄色统计数字共同构成品牌识别。

查询.sql ^ clickhouse
-- Aggregate billions of rows in milliseconds
SELECT
  toDate(event_time) AS day,
  uniqExact(user_id) AS users,
  count() AS events
FROM analytics
WHERE event_time >= '2026-01-01'
GROUP BY day
ORDER BY day DESC
LIMIT 100;
