字体排版

Two display registers run side by side — Oracle Sans at weight 300 on the corporate index, and Georgia across the product pages. Letter-spacing is zero on every role at every size. Inter stands in for Oracle Sans; Georgia is shown as itself.

层次与阴影

系统根本就没有影子 深度完全由中性梯度上的位置产生,其中两个表面共用一个径的1px 细边框则进行分离.（原文参数：1px）

Level 0 — flat fill on the ladder. Every band, section, nav and footer.
Level 1 — one rung up or down. Cards on a tinted band, the deep sub-navigation strip.
职等 2 — a 1px 细边线,两个表面共用一个径. 导航牌和禁用控制器。
