触控区域

  • Primary CTAs at minimum 48 × 48px (above WCAG AAA).
  • Search orb is 48 × 48px circular — the most-tapped element on the page.
  • Heart save button 32 × 32px circular — borderline AAA, compensated by 12px photo padding.
  • Date-picker day cells are 40 × 40px circular.

窄屏布局调整

  • 顶级产品标签会倒塌成744px以下的汉堡单.（原文参数：744px）
  • Search bar's 3 segments collapse into a single-tap entry on mobile.
  • 财产和城市网格的降列数清净——永远不回流行.
  • Reservation card switches from sticky right-rail to sticky bottom bar on mobile.
